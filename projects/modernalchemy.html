Modern Alchemist is a website that reinterprets alchemy, presenting a modern, evidence-based perspective on early scientific practices while challenging common myths of gold-making and immortality.

Using the visual language of lab reports and scientific experimentation, the site employs clean, minimal, and clinical design to highlight content, while experimenting with unconventional text, image, and caption formatting to enhance engagement.
