'His Design for Life' interprets the design philosophy of László Moholy-Nagy through a contemporary editorial format.

Drawing on Bauhaus principles of structure, clarity, and experimentation, the publication uses form, shape and typography to create visually disruptive spreads that move the viewer through the piece.
