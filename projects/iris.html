'Iris' logo branding for casual eyewear brand. Humourous and light-hearted, as evidenced by the image created when logo is rotated.

The logo was first designed to fit within a circular shape; the face was found later once editing. 'Iris' is a reference to the coloured part of the eye that controls pupil size.
