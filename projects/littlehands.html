'Little Hands' is a colourful, illustrated AUSLAN card deck designed to introduce children aged 5–7 to Australian Sign Language in a fun and interactive way.

By encouraging playful exploration, teamwork, and active engagement, the cards help young learners build confidence while connecting meaningfully with the deaf community.
