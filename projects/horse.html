'You can lead a horse to water but it may drown' is a two-part poster publication made to accompany a short essay written by myself, which explores clickbait and abundance in the modern age.

The design elements hope to mirror the digitised natural world as we see it through our screens, using monotype and script fonts, as well as pixelated effects.
