'I.AM.AI' is a university-led project involved designing and directing a social campaign aimed at motivating community awareness and change.

The campaign explores the environmental and social implications of artificial intelligence, encouraging critical discussion and a renewed prioritisation of human connection over emotional reliance on AI.
